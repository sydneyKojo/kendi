Conversation Guidelines Template

Start with a Warm Greeting; Make the other person feel comfortable and welcomed.

  • Example: "Hi, how's your day been so far?"

Light Small Talk, Spend 1 to 2 minutes on easy, casual topics before diving deeper.

  • Example: "How's your week going?"

Transition to the Main Topic, Gently lead into the actual topic without making it feel sudden.

  • Example: "I was thinking about {{topic.description}} and I'm curious what you think"

Deepen the Conversation, After initial answers, you can go deeper with "why" and "how" question.

  • Example: "How did that 'answer' change the way you think about {{topic.title}}?"

Wrap Up Gently, After a good conversation, slow it down and end positively.

  • Example: "I feel like I understand you better after talking about this."
